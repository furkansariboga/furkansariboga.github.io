🧠 What is AYIK?

AYIK is a private, on-device mobile app designed to help you regain control by tracking the exact time passed since a habit's last occurrence.

“Bad habit recovery should not be monetized.”
— Core project philosophy (Inspired by the open-source project Sobriety).

Unlike modern tracking apps bloated with subscriptions, cloud telemetry, paywalls, and advertisements, AYIK was built on the core belief that your recovery journey is strictly personal and should never be profited off.

🔒 100% Private & On-Device

Your personal habits and struggles are nobody else's business:

✔ No Cloud Sync / No Servers: All your habit data, timestamps, and notes live strictly inside your phone's local Room Database.
✔ Zero Analytics & Zero Trackers: No Google Analytics, no Facebook SDKs, no behavioral telemetry.
✔ 100% Offline: Works without an internet connection. No sign-up, no login, and no email needed.
✔ App Lock & Export: Lock your sensitive trackers and export your data at any time.

Read our full AYIK Privacy Policy for more details.

⚡ Features

Everything you need to stay on track:

▶ Real-Time Milestones & Streaks

✔ Live ticking counter measuring days, hours, minutes, and seconds since your last occurrence.
✔ Track multiple habits simultaneously with custom titles and starting points.
✔ Relapse logging with timestamps to maintain an honest and reflective record.
✔ “Mark Day” feature to reinforce everyday wins.

▶ Financial & Time Savings Calculator

✔ Money Saved: Set your estimated daily cost and watch your cumulative savings grow in real time.
✔ Time Reclaimed: Enter daily minutes lost to the habit and quantify how many hours and days you've won back.

▶ Statistics & Clean UI

✔ Simple, modern, and uncluttered Material design interface.
✔ Dedicated statistics views to analyze patterns and long-term improvements.
✔ Free, open source, and lightweight.
